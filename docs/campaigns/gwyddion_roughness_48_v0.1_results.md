# Gwyddion roughness 48-case results v0.1

The frozen 48-case campaign completed with 48 of 48 cases and 144 of 144 Sa,
Sq, and Sz comparisons within the accepted atol 1e-6 nm plus rtol 1e-6 rule.
The largest absolute delta was 1.617905809325748e-11 nm and the largest
threshold ratio was 4.071062797544526e-07.

Inputs were regenerated from the frozen design using the listed SPMKit and
Phantoms commits. Every canonical matrix and GSF hash matched the freeze.
SPMKit statistics and the Gwyddion 2.71 native GSF/libgwyprocess route used
the same float32 nm payload without preprocessing or normalization scaling.

This is LEVEL 3 CROSS_VALIDATED evidence for Sa, Sq, and Sz on these frozen
matrices only. It is not physical validation, instrumental validation, or
validation of all SPMKit algorithms.
